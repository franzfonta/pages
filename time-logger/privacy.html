time-logger · Privacy policy

time-logger is a personal tool used only by its author, Francesco Amedeo Fontana. This page describes what it does with Google user data.

What it reads

  • Google Calendar, read only: the calendars of the author's account and their events (time, title, attendees, the author's response, the meeting link).
  • Gmail, metadata only: date, recipients and subject of sent messages. It never reads the body of any message.

How it uses and stores the data

The data is used only to show the author how he spent his working time. It is stored on a computer in the author's home and is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone. No data is used for advertising, and none is used to train AI models.

time-logger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. The stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen of time-logger.
